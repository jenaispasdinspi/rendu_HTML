A GUIDE TO FIND THE BEST DPS TOWER

DPS towers are an important part of TDS. Any mode as hard or harder than Molten mode require at least one DPS tower. There are plenty of choices and sometimes its not really obvious which one you should take. Thankfully, this guide will show you all the factors that determines how good a DPS tower is, from how hard it is to acquire to how good it is at other aspects such as mid game capabilities or support. This guide will put in clash 8 DPS towers that will then be ranked in different categories such as overall DPSs, solo play, team play, unlock difficulty, detections, support and then combine all these factors to find the best DPS tower.
